ABOUT

Francisco Hernandez, founder of Cashflow Cartel.

Francisco is an operator first. He runs real businesses in Northwest Arkansas — and he built Cashflow Cartel for the owner he knows best: the one whose business depends on them for everything.

When every decision, every sale, and every problem runs through the owner, the business stops growing at the speed of one person. Cashflow Cartel exists to break that pattern — not with more tactics, but by finding the one constraint that is actually choking the business and fixing that first.

Francisco is the author of HYPER-FOCUSED — The Entrepreneur's Operating System for Doing Less, Executing Better & Moving the Business Forward, the book that lays out the full system the Cartel runs on. The free Constraint Finder guide is an excerpt from it.

The operating philosophy

Four ideas run everything inside the Cartel:

  • Find the #1 constraint Every business has one bottleneck that limits all the others. Name it honestly before touching anything else.
  • Fix that first One thing at a time, in the right order. Spreading effort across ten problems moves none of them.
  • One proof number Pick the single number that proves the constraint is loosening — and watch that number, not vanity metrics.
  • Friday receipts Every week ends with a receipt: what got done, what the number says, and what happens next. No receipts, no progress.

THE BOOK

HYPER-FOCUSED

The Entrepreneur's Operating System for Doing Less, Executing Better & Moving the Business Forward — by Francisco Hernandez, founder of Cashflow Cartel.

Most owners don't have an effort problem. They have a focus problem — too many priorities, all moving slowly. HYPER-FOCUSED is the operating system Francisco built to run his own businesses: five parts that take an owner from an honest look at reality to a weekly rhythm of execution and review.

  1. 01

    Direction

    Current Reality · 90-Day Outcome · 30-Day Sprint — know exactly where you are, where you're going, and what this month is for.

  2. 02

    Focus

    #1 Constraint · One Thing · Proof Number · Focus Firewall — narrow everything down to the one constraint and protect the work on it.

  3. 03

    Make Room

    Before anything new gets added, what doesn't serve the outcome comes off the plate.

  4. 04

    Execute

    Work the sprint in deliberate moves — the plan becomes daily and weekly action, not a document.

  5. 05

    Review

    Operator Standard · Friday Receipt — hold the standard, close every week with a receipt, and let the numbers set the next move.
